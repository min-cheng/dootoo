Open dootoo dootoo.vercel.app
About 5 minutes

Get set up

  1. On your phone, open dootoo.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Sign up. Enter your email, pick a password of at least 6 characters, and tap Create account.
  3. If an email arrives asking you to confirm, tap the link in it, then come back and sign in.
  4. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen. dootoo now opens like any other app.

On Android, use Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen.

The main thing

Add a task

Tap the purple + in the bottom corner and type the task the way you'd say it. dootoo picks out dates, times and a few shortcuts for you.

Dates it understands: today, tomorrow, next week, a day like friday, or a date like june 15 (spell out the month). Times: at 3pm or at 3:30pm.

Just in case

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and tap Email me a reset link. Open the email, tap the link, and choose a new password. You'll land right back in your list.

No email after a few minutes? Check spam. If it's not there, wait an hour and try again.
